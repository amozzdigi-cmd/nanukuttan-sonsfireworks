Our Story

A Legacy Lit in 1956

Nanukuttan & Sons began as one man’s trade in a small Kerala town — and grew, festival by festival, into a name three generations of families have welcomed into their celebrations. Here’s how that flame has travelled.

Late Mr. Nanukuttan — Founder

“Every box that leaves our store still carries his name — and his promise.”

1956

The First Spark

The late Mr. Nanukuttan begins trading fireworks in Kattanam, building his name one honest sale at a time.

1970s – 2010s

A Name Kerala Trusted

Word spread from village to village. Families returned year after year for Onam, Perunnal, Christmas, temple festivals, and every celebration in between — and later brought their children back with them.

Today

Third Generation, Same Promise

His sons now run the business, pairing their father’s standards with modern, professional trading practices — so every celebration is still lit with care.
